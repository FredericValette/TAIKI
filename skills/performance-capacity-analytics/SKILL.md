# Performance and Capacity Analytics

Assess measured system behavior and capacity risks for an explicitly defined workload and time window.

## Workflow

1. Confirm service scope, workload profile, time window, and service objectives.
2. Validate units, sampling, aggregation, and data completeness before comparing results.
3. Analyze latency, throughput, saturation, errors, and capacity headroom where available.
4. Report bottlenecks, forecast assumptions, and testable actions with evidence.

Do not extrapolate beyond the observed workload without stating assumptions and uncertainty. Use the prompt and output contract in this directory.
