# Technology Radar

Assess technology choices against use cases, organizational constraints, lifecycle, and evidence quality.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the decision context, evaluation horizon, and non-negotiable constraints.
2. Compare candidate technologies using consistent criteria and dated sources.
3. Separate facts, vendor claims, assumptions, and team experience.
4. Recommend a radar disposition with confidence, risks, and review date.

Do not treat popularity or vendor marketing as proof of fit. Use the prompt and output contract in this directory.
